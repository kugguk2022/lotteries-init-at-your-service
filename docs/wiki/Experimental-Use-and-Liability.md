## What “experimental” means

Every surface is included: source repository, PyPI package, library, CLI, HTTP API, local mini app,
models, strategies, datasets, benchmarks, examples, reports, and generated portfolios. Any of them
may contain errors, stale third-party data, unstable interfaces, incorrect assumptions, or
incomplete country support. They may change or disappear without notice.

Tests establish only that specified behaviour passed in a particular environment. They do not
certify accuracy, security, profitability, regulatory compliance, production readiness, or fitness
for a user's purpose. Likewise, backtests and prospective ledgers are experiments—not warranties or
evidence that future draws can be predicted.

## How outputs must be interpreted

- A generated portfolio is a reproducible research sample, not a recommendation to buy it.
- A score or ranking is relative output from a strategy, not a probability of winning.
- Coverage can be measured, but it does not make any individual fair-draw ticket more likely.
- Popularity analysis can discuss prize sharing conditional on a win; it cannot create a win.
- Expected return for ordinary lottery participation is negative. This project does not establish a
  positive-return strategy.

Do not describe LottoBench output as “winning numbers”, “safe”, “guaranteed”, “profitable”,
“approved”, or “financial advice”. Applications built on LottoBench should display the standing
disclaimer next to any generated output rather than hiding it in terms or documentation.

## Product boundary

LottoBench does not accept money, sell or buy tickets, execute wagers, pool stakes, hold balances, verify
identity or age, or determine official results. Adding those capabilities would materially change
the legal, security, and regulatory character of the project and is outside its scope.

Country and operator names identify game definitions and third-party data sources. They do not imply
affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship, or official status. Always verify rules and results with the
relevant official operator.
