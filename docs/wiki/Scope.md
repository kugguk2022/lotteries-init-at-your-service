# Project Scope

This project provides a research framework for studying lottery data and fixed-budget candidate
portfolios in simulation.

## Included

- Historical draw collection, normalization, and validation.
- Candidate generation and deterministic portfolio aggregation.
- Forward-only, equal-budget comparison against matched controls.
- Coverage, diversity, hit-tier, payout, net-return, and jackpot-sharing analysis.
- Reproducible pre-draw records and settlement against official outcomes.

## Excluded

- Pooling or holding funds.
- Buying tickets or executing wagers.
- Operating a betting service.
- Guaranteed-winnings claims or presenting an unvalidated edge as established.

## Evidence requirements

Prediction and positive-return performance remain research questions. Results count only when the
method, budget, evaluation window, and control are recorded. Prospective and forward-only results
take precedence over in-sample fit, and negative or inconclusive outcomes remain in the record.
